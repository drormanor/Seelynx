# Code of conduct

Seelynx should be a friendly project to take part in, whoever you are and whatever your
experience.

## Expected

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Discuss the work, not the person. Disagree with reasons.
- Accept a decision once it's made, even if it wasn't yours.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language
- Personal attacks, or threatening anyone
- Publishing someone's private information, such as their address or camera feeds
- Sexual language or images
- Repeatedly disrupting discussions

## Where it applies

In issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space, and when you represent the
project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainer through the contact details on the maintainer's
GitHub profile. Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, and warn, temporarily block or permanently ban
anyone who breaks this code.
